Lookout lets you keep photos inside the app and choose people ("watchers") who get a text
message if a photo is shared, downloaded, screenshotted or deleted.

## What we store

**If you use the app**
- Your phone number, used to sign in.
- The name you enter, shown to your watchers in texts.
- The photos you upload. They are resized to at most 1600 pixels and stripped of location
  and other embedded data before they leave your phone.
- For each photo: whether downloads are allowed, and which watchers you chose.
- The names and phone numbers of the watchers you add.
- A history of events on your photos (shared, downloaded, screenshotted, deleted, watcher
  added or removed) and of the texts sent on your behalf.

**If someone added you as a watcher**
- Your phone number and the name they typed for you.
- Whether you confirmed your number, and whether you opted out.
- The texts sent to you.

Watchers never see the photos. Lookout does not read your camera roll: it only receives the
photos you pick, and only deletes originals if you ask it to and confirm on your iPhone.

## What we use it for

Only to run the app: signing you in, storing your photos privately, and texting your
watchers. We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use your photos for anything
else.

## Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** hosts the database and the photo files.
- **Twilio** delivers the text messages, so it receives the recipient's phone number and
  the message.
- **Expo** delivers app updates and does not receive your photos or contacts.

## Watchers: confirming and opting out

A watcher is only texted alerts after confirming with the 6-digit code sent to their phone.
A watcher can reply **STOP** to any Lookout text to stop all texts, and **START** to allow
them again. Message and data rates may apply.

## Deleting your data

- Delete a photo in the app and its file is removed; its watchers are told.
- **Account → Delete account** removes your photos, watchers and history. Your confirmed
  watchers get one text saying the account was deleted.
- A watcher who wants their number removed can reply STOP, or contact us.

## Children

Lookout is not intended for children under 13.
